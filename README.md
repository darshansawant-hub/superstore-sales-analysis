# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project explores Superstore retail transaction data using Python to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, products, regions, and shipping modes.

The goal is to turn raw sales data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales, profit, orders, and customer activity.
- Compare product categories and sub-categories.
- Identify regional and state-level profitability patterns.
- Examine monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Evaluate customer segments and shipping modes.
- Identify products and locations associated with losses.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab
- Excel

## Project Workflow
1. Loaded and inspected the dataset.
2. Checked missing values, duplicate records, and date validity.
3. Converted order and shipping dates to datetime format.
4. Created order month and order year features.
5. Calculated key performance indicators.
6. Conducted exploratory data analysis.
7. Created charts and a sales and profitability dashboard.
8. Developed business insights and recommendations.

## Key Findings
- Total sales: approximately $2.33 million.
- Total profit: approximately $292,297.
- Overall profit margin: approximately 12.56%.
- Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the main categories.
- Furniture had a comparatively low profit margin of 2.61%.
- The West was the strongest region by total sales and profit.
- Consumer customers contributed the highest total sales and profit.
- Standard Class generated the most sales among shipping modes.
- Texas and Pennsylvania recorded negative total profit in the state-level analysis.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate discounting, pricing, and costs for loss-making products and states.
- Review Furniture profitability and identify opportunities to improve margins.
- Investigate the relatively low profit margin in the Central region.
- Study high-performing products to understand their contribution to profitability.
- Validate date records before using yearly trends for business decisions.

## Data Quality & Limitations
The dataset contains order dates extending to December 30, 2026, later than the analysis date of October 18, 2026. These future-dated records require validation before final conclusions are drawn from monthly or yearly trends.

This project is a descriptive analysis of the supplied dataset. Observed patterns do not necessarily establish cause and effect.
